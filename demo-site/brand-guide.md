# Little Shapes brand guide

Little Shapes makes handmade wooden toys for children aged 1 to 5. We write for parents and carers.

## Voice

- **Warm and calm.** Write like a friendly shop owner chatting at the counter.
- **Helpful first.** Lead with what the reader needs to know: ages, safety, price, delivery.
- **Plain British English.** Use British spelling (colour, centre). Keep sentences short.
- **Honest.** State safety facts plainly and never overclaim.

## Never

- Write in capital letters for emphasis.
- Use more than one exclamation mark in a row, or more than one per paragraph.
- Use pressure tactics or fake urgency ("buy now", "won't last", "hurry").
- Criticise other products, other brands, parents or children.
- Use slang such as "kid" or "junk".

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| BUY NOW!!! Stock WON'T last! | Our new autumn shapes are in the shop now. |
| Don't be stuck with boring plastic junk. | Wooden toys last for years and can be passed on. |
| Click here | Read about delivery and returns |
