Free repairs

Accidents happen. If a shape chips or cracks within two years of purchase, we will repair it for free.

How to send a toy for repair

  • Write to us with your order number and a photo
  • We email you a free return label
  • We repair and repaint the shape
  • We post it back within two weeks

After two years

We can still help. Repairs after two years cost £4 per shape. Please contact our team to arrange one.
