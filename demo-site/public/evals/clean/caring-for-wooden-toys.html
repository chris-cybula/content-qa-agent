Caring for wooden toys

Wooden toys are easy to look after. A little care keeps the paint bright and the wood smooth.

Cleaning

Wipe the shapes with a damp cloth and a drop of mild soap. Let them dry naturally. Please do not soak them or put them in the dishwasher.

Storing

Keep the shapes somewhere dry and away from radiators, as strong heat can make wood crack.

Scratches and chips

Small marks are part of a well-loved toy. For deeper chips, see our free repair service.
