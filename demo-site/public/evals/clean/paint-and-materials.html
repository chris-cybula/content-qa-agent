Our wood and paint

We use two materials: beech wood and water-based paint. Here is where they come from and why we chose them.

Beech wood

Beech is hard and close-grained, so it does not splinter easily. Ours comes from managed forests and carries the FSC mark.

Water-based paint

Our paints are tested to the UK toy safety standard for children's toys. They contain no solvents, and we let every coat dry for a full day.

Finishing

Each shape is sanded three times before painting. You can see this on a workshop visit.
