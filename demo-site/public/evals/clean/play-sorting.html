Play idea: sorting by colour

Sorting is one of the first games toddlers enjoy. You only need three shapes and three bowls.

Setting up

Put the red circle, blue square and green triangle in front of your child, with a bowl for each colour.

Playing

Name each colour as your child places a shape. Let them lead and change the rules if they want to.

Making it harder

When sorting by colour feels easy, try sorting by shape or by size instead.
