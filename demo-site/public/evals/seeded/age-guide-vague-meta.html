Choosing shapes by age

All our shapes are made for children from 12 months to 5 years. Some work better at certain ages, so here is how we would choose.

From 12 months

Start with one large shape, such as the red circle. Babies enjoy holding, turning and dropping it.

From 2 years

Add shapes with flat sides for stacking, like the blue square, and the orange stacking set.

From 3 years

Mix colours and shapes for sorting and pattern games. The striped block is good for counting.
