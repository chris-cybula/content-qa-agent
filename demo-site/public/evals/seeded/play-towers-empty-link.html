Play idea: building towers

Stacking builds balance, patience and hand control. Here is how to start.

A first stack

Begin with two orange squares. Flat shapes on flat shapes are the easiest to balance.

Taller towers

Add one square at a time. Wide shapes at the bottom make the tower steadier.

Adding a roof

A green triangle on top turns a tower into a house. When it falls, build it again together.
