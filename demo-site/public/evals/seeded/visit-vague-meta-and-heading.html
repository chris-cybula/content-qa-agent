Visit the workshop

On the first Saturday of every month we open the workshop to visitors.

What happens on a visit

  • A short tour of the cutting and sanding area
  • A chance to paint your own shape
  • Tea and squash in the garden

Info

Visits are free and last about an hour. Places are limited to ten people, so please book through our contact page.

Getting here

The workshop is a ten-minute walk from Totnes station. There is parking for four cars.
