Sustainability

We want our toys to last for years and be passed on. Here is how we try to keep our impact low.

Wood

All our beech carries the FSC mark, which means it comes from forests that are replanted.

Offcuts

Unlike the cheap plastic junk other shops sell, our toys won't be in the bin after a week. Other brands should be ashamed of themselves.

Packaging

We post orders in recycled cardboard with paper tape, so everything can go in your recycling bin.

Repairs

Fixing a toy is better than replacing it, so we offer free repairs for two years.
