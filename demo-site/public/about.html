About Little Shapes

We are a team of four makers who started Little Shapes in 2019, after struggling to find simple wooden toys that were not made of plastic.

Our workshop

Our workshop sits on the edge of Dartmoor. Each shape is cut on a bandsaw, sanded by hand and finished with two coats of paint.

Our materials

We use beech from managed forests and water-based paints that meet the EN 71-3 toy safety standard.
