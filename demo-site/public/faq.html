Frequently asked questions

Which ages are the shapes for?

Our shapes suit children from 12 months to 5 years. Every piece is too large to be a choking hazard.

How do I clean them?

Wipe them with a damp cloth and let them dry naturally. Please do not soak them or put them in the dishwasher.
